Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters to Us

Effective Date: May 7, 2025  |  Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to e-clock.top's Privacy Policy — At e-clock.top, your privacy and digital security matter deeply. This Privacy Policy outlines how information is managed across all of our digital timekeeping and productivity services, including the e-clock.top website and the official Focus Pomodoro Timer & Website Blocker - by e-clock.top browser extension. By accessing or using our website or extension, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

What We Do NOT Collect

🚫 No Personal Information

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, physical address, email, or telephone number.

🆓 No Account Required

Our tools are freely accessible without mandatory user registration, accounts, passwords, or personal profiles.

🌐 No Browsing History Collection

We do not record, log, or sell your web browsing history, search queries, or visited web pages.

⌨️ No Keystroke or Content Logging

We do not monitor or log keystrokes, personal input fields, passwords, or communications.

Local Storage & On-Device Processing

Your Preferences Stay on Your Device — Custom user settings (including timers, alarms, custom website blocklists, themes, focus schedules, and free activation passes) are stored locally on your device using standard browser storage (localStorage and chrome.storage.local). This data is processed on-device and is never transmitted to our servers or sold to third parties.

How We Use Permissions & Features

To provide accurate timekeeping, focus sessions, and voluntary distraction blocking, our services utilize the following minimal capabilities:

  • Focus Timer & Website Blocking: While a focus session is active, the extension checks website domains locally on your computer strictly to verify if they match your personal blocklist. This evaluation happens entirely on your machine; no URLs or browsing logs are sent to external servers.
  • Desktop Notifications: Used purely locally to alert you when your focus period or break time has concluded.
  • Side Panel & Display: Used to display the Pomodoro timer and clock tools within your browser window.

Website Analytics & Cookies

We use Google Analytics on our website to understand anonymous, aggregated visitor traffic trends and ensure our services perform reliably:

  • Aggregated page visits and tool usage statistics

  • Coarse, non-identifying geographic region (e.g., city-level)

Note: Analytics are limited to the website and do not track private browsing or personal identity. You can opt out of Google Analytics at any time using Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Third-Party Services

We connect with select third-party services to deliver live clock, weather, and operational features across our platform:

📊 Google Analytics

For anonymous web traffic monitoring, processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

🕐 timeapi.io

Synchronizes atomic timestamps for accurate world clocks. No personal information is sent or stored.

🌍 ipapi.co

Identifies approximate city-level location to automatically load local timezones and weather data.

🌤️ Open-Meteo

Fetches meteorological forecasts based on general coordinates. No personal identity data is shared or retained.

Our software strictly complies with browser developer policies, including the Google Chrome Web Store User Data and Limited Use requirements:
