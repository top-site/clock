Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters to Us

Effective Date: May 7, 2025  |  Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to e-clock.top's Privacy Policy — At e-clock.top, your privacy and digital security matter deeply. This Privacy Policy covers all of our digital time management and productivity services, including the e-clock.top website and the official Focus Pomodoro Timer & Website Blocker - by e-clock.top browser extension. By using our website or extension, you agree to the transparent data practices described below.

What We Do NOT Collect

🚫 No Personal Information

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, physical location, or phone number.

🆓 No Registration Required

Our tools are completely free to use without any account creation, passwords, sign-ups, or user profiles.

🌐 No Browsing History Collection

We do not track, record, store, transmit, or sell your web browsing history, search queries, or visited web pages.

⌨️ No Keystroke or Content Logging

We do not log your keystrokes, input field contents, passwords, or personal communications.

Local Storage & On-Device Processing

Your Data Stays on Your Device — All custom preferences, alarms, timers, theme settings, focus durations, custom website blocklists, and free activation passes are stored 100% locally on your device using standard browser storage (localStorage and chrome.storage.local). This information is strictly processed on your own computer and is never transmitted to our servers or shared with any third party.

How We Use Permissions & Data

To provide accurate timekeeping, productivity focus timers, and voluntary website blocking, our tools use the following minimal on-device capabilities:

  • Focus Timer & Shielding: While an active focus timer is running, our blocker inspects URL hostnames locally on your device solely to check if they match websites in your personal blocklist. This check happens entirely on your machine; no URLs or browsing logs ever leave your browser.
  • Desktop Notifications: Used purely locally to alert you when your focus period or break time has concluded.
  • Side Panel & Display: Used to render our clean clock and Pomodoro timer interface within your browser.

Analytics & Cookies

We use Google Analytics on our website to understand anonymous, aggregated visitor traffic and keep our free services running smoothly:

  • Aggregated page visits and tool usage statistics
  • General device, screen, and browser types
  • Coarse, non-identifying geographic region (e.g., city-level)

Note: Analytics are limited to the website and do not track your personal identity or private browsing. You can opt out of Google Analytics at any time using Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Third-Party Services

We connect with a small number of trusted, privacy-focused third-party APIs to deliver live time and weather data across our platform:

📊 Google Analytics

For anonymous web performance monitoring and traffic analysis, processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

🕐 timeapi.io

Used to synchronize precise world clock times. No personal data is sent or stored.

🌍 ipapi.co

Retrieves a non-identifying, approximate city-level location to automatically load local weather and timezone data.

🌤️ Open-Meteo

Fetches weather forecasts based on general coordinates. No personal identity data is shared or retained.

Advertising & Data Broker Guarantee: We do not partner with advertising networks, behavioral trackers, or data brokers. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your data under any circumstance.

Data Security & Protection

🔒 HTTPS Encryption

All connections to e-clock.top use secure HTTPS encryption to protect data integrity in transit.

🛡️ Browser Sandboxing

All tool preferences and blocking rules operate strictly within secure browser sandboxes isolated on your machine.

Limited Use & Platform Compliance

Our tools comply strictly with browser developer platform policies, including Google Chrome Web Store User Data and Limited Use policies:

  • We only access capabilities necessary to provide our digital clock, focus timer, and website blocker functions.
  • We never transfer or sell user data to third parties.
  • We never use data for personalized advertising, credit assessment, or user profiling.

Your Rights & Choices

  • Clear All Local Data: You can clear your browser storage, cookies, or remove the extension at any time to instantly erase all saved timers, blocklists, and settings.
  • Opt-Out of Analytics: Use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or enable "Do Not Track" in your browser settings.
  • Control Permissions: You can adjust extension permissions anytime via your browser's extension settings.

Policy Updates
