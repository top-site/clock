About Singapore Standard Time (SGT / UTC+8)

Singapore operates on Singapore Standard Time (SGT), which is 8 hours ahead of Coordinated Universal Time (UTC+8) year-round. Because Singapore is located just 1.35° north of the equator, daylight hours remain remarkably consistent throughout the year (sunrise around 6:50–7:15 AM and sunset around 6:50–7:15 PM), and Singapore does not observe Daylight Saving Time.

Same UTC+8 Timezone Corridor

Singapore shares the exact same UTC+8 clock time with Beijing, Shanghai, Hong Kong, Taipei, Kuala Lumpur, Manila, Brunei, Bali/Makassar, and Perth (Western Australia). Best Cross-Border Meeting Windows from Singapore

  • Singapore ↔ London / Europe: 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM SGT corresponds to 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM GMT (winter) or 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM BST (summer). Compare live on our London Time page.
  • Singapore ↔ New York / US East Coast: 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM SGT (7:00 PM – 9:00 PM EST / 8:00 PM – 10:00 PM EDT) or 9:00 PM – 11:00 PM SGT (8:00 AM – 10:00 AM EST). Compare live on our New York Time page.
  • Singapore ↔ Los Angeles / Silicon Valley: 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM SGT corresponds to 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM PST (5:00 PM – 8:00 PM PDT). Compare live on our Los Angeles Time page.
  • Singapore ↔ Tokyo / Sydney / India: Tokyo is +1h ahead of SGT; Sydney is +2h/+3h ahead of SGT; India (IST) is 2 hours 30 minutes behind SGT.

Does Singapore ever change its clocks for Daylight Saving Time?

No. Singapore stays on UTC+8 all year. When comparing SGT with London, New York, or Sydney, any seasonal shift in time difference is caused by those countries entering or leaving Daylight Saving Time.
